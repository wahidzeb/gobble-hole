# Gobble Hole by Zaib

Drag a hungry black hole around a colourful low-poly 3D world and swallow everything smaller than you: cones, benches, people, cars, trees, lamp posts, buses and, eventually, whole buildings. Every bite makes the hole bigger.

**Play:** https://zaibgames.com/gobble-hole/

## Modes
- **Classic**: 2-minute rounds against 7 AI holes. Bigger holes eat smaller ones, there's a live leaderboard, and the highest score wins.
- **Solo**: the map is all yours. Eat 50% / 80% / 100% for 1-3 stars.
- **Battle**: 6 holes, no respawns, and a shrinking safe zone. Be the last hole standing.

## Features
- 4 maps: Sunny City, Coco Beach, Hayday Farm and Orbit Station
- About 50 kinds of objects that tip over and fall in (buildings sink with a shake)
- 13 hole skins and 9 trails to buy with coins, plus 3 daily missions and 16 achievements
- Interactive tutorial, settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics), pause and results
- Raw WebGL renderer: instanced draws, stencil-cut holes, grid culling, x-ray outline for hidden holes, automatic quality fallback
- Synthesized WebAudio music and sound effects with a master limiter
- Progress saved in localStorage

## Controls
- Touch/mouse: drag anywhere (relative joystick)
- Keyboard: WASD / arrow keys, P or Esc to pause

Single self-contained `index.html`, no external libraries.
