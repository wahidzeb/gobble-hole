How to play

Drag anywhere (or use WASD / arrows) to move your hole.

Anything smaller than the hole tips over and falls in. Every bite makes you bigger, so you can swallow cars, buses and finally whole buildings.

Classic: 2 minutes against 7 rival holes. A bigger hole can eat a smaller one, so hunt the little ones and run from the giants. Highest score wins.

Solo: the city is all yours. Eat as much as you can before time runs out: 50% for ★, 80% for ★★, 100% for ★★★.

Battle: no respawns, and the safe zone shrinks. Stay inside it and be the last hole standing.

Earn coins for skins and trails, and finish daily missions for bonus coins.
