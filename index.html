About Gobble Hole

In Gobble Hole you are a hungry hole in the ground. Slide under cones, people and cars to swallow them, grow bigger, and eventually sink whole skyscrapers.

In Classic you race seven computer holes to be the biggest when the two-minute timer runs out. Solo asks you to eat as much of the map as you can, and Battle keeps shrinking the safe zone until one hole is left.

3 quick tips

  1. Start on the pavements. People, cones, bins and benches are quick to collect.
  2. Do not chase things you cannot eat yet. If a car just bumps the rim, come back after your next size-up.
  3. Save buildings for last. They are worth a lot, but you need to be large first.

How to play

Drag anywhere (or use WASD / arrows) to move your hole.

Anything smaller than the hole tips over and falls in. Every bite makes you bigger, so you can swallow cars, buses and finally whole buildings.

Classic: 2 minutes against 7 rival holes. A bigger hole can eat a smaller one, so hunt the little ones and run from the giants. Highest score wins.

Solo: the city is all yours. Eat as much as you can before time runs out: 50% for ★, 80% for ★★, 100% for ★★★.

Battle: no respawns, and the safe zone shrinks. Stay inside it and be the last hole standing.

Earn coins for skins and trails, and finish daily missions for bonus coins.
